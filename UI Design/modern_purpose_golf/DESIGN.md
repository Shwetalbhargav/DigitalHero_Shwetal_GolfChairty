## Brand & Style

This design system serves a modern, purpose-led golf subscription platform uniting athletic development, charitable giving, and recurring prize incentives. The brand philosophy—"Feel, not fairway"—abandons tired country club tropes, tartan motifs, crests, and aggressive casino gamification. Instead, it frames the experience around intentionality, craft, calm confidence, and collective impact. 

The visual narrative prioritizes purpose first (grassroots impact and environmental sustainability), athletic progression second (performance metrics and coaching access), and monthly prize events third. 

The aesthetic blends **Modern Organic Minimalism** with **Tactile Frosted Glassmorphism**:
- Generous breathing room, warm ivory canvases, and botanical-rich dark greens create grounded stability.
- Champagne gold is strictly governed: reserved exclusively for winnings, impact milestones, and celebratory moments to prevent visual fatigue.
- Frosted translucent layers and gossamer sage boundaries bring a soft, tactile precision reminiscent of premium technical apparel and high-performance equipment.

## Colors

The system relies on an intentional hierarchy that preserves emotional resonance and clarity:

- **Primary (`#1B4D3E` — Deep Grass Green)**: The core brand anchor. Used for primary actions, strong focal anchors, and prominent branded card structures.
- **Secondary (`#0D2E24` — Dark Forest Green)**: Deep grounding surface color. Applied to persistent navigation frames, dark mode surface layers, hero banners, and high-contrast editorial blocks.
- **Tertiary (`#C99A45` — Champagne Gold)**: Reserved strictly as a reward and celebration indicator. It is used for jackpot counters, winning status markers, tier achievements, and impact milestones. Never use this color for standard UI interactions, dismissals, or non-draw buttons.
- **Neutral (`#19211D` — Deep Charcoal Text)**: Replaces harsh black to maintain a soft editorial tone across high-contrast typography, icons, and structure.
- **Background (`#FBFBF7` — Warm Ivory)**: Canvas tone that prevents sterile digital white, providing an organic, editorial paper feel.
- **Surface Light (`#E6EDE8` — Soft Sage)**: Subtle fill for containers, inactive states, table rows, and secondary backgrounds.
- **Outline (`#D1DED5` — Sage Border)**: Low-contrast boundary tone designed for hair-thin translucent and solid dividing lines.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Outfit** for structural clarity with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for functional prose:

- **Display & Headlines (Outfit)**: Conveys contemporary sport luxury. Set with tightened letter-spacing on display tiers to retain density and athletic momentum, loosening slightly as hierarchy steps down.
- **Body, Form Controls & Badges (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Ensures instant scanning of metrics, draw allocations, and narrative editorial impact statements. Generous line-heights guard legibility across high-density data views.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: Metric counters, yardage data, and draw allocations utilize proportional tabular numbers where vertical alignment is required.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptive 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by strict vertical rhythm:

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column fluid grid, `3rem` section margins, `1.5rem` column gutters. Maximum content width caps at `1280px` for optimal reading comfort.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Primary sidebars collapse into drawer states or sub-headers.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` column gutters. Bottom navigation bar takes dominance over side navigation.
- **Component Padding Scale**: Internal spacing strictly abides by an 8-point harmonic rhythm. Micro-spacing (`0.25rem`–`0.5rem`) handles tag padding, metadata, and icon-to-label gaps; structural spacing (`1rem`–`2.5rem`) sets card interior margins and section flow.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphism, soft ambient shadows, and gossamer boundary lines rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (Ground)**: Warm Ivory (`#FBFBF7`) canvas with zero elevation.
- **Floating Surfaces (Cards, Modules)**: Elevated via semi-translucent soft sage backing (`rgba(230, 237, 232, 0.75)` on light mode, `rgba(13, 46, 36, 0.85)` on dark mode), enhanced with a `12px` to `16px` backdrop-filter blur and a 1px boundary stroke using `#D1DED5` (or `rgba(209, 222, 213, 0.15)` against dark forest tones).
- **Ambient Shadow System**: When floating over photography or complex performance dashboards, cards cast an ultra-soft atmospheric shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(13, 46, 36, 0.06)`.
- **Modals & Overlays**: Grounded by a backdrop scrim tinted with `#0D2E24` at `40%` opacity and a `6px` gaussian blur. The elevated modal surface carries a stronger ambient drop: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(13, 46, 36, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects organic precision—smooth curves echoing the contour of fairways and aerodynamic equipment:

- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` to `rounded-xl` / `1.5rem`)**: Surfaces, metric widgets, and performance modules use continuous rounded edges to feel approachable and tactile.
- **Interactive Controls & Number Chips (`rounded-full`)**: Primary buttons, input pill fields, status chips, and interactive draw-entry number tokens use full-radius pill styling.
- **Modals & Bottom Drawers**: Desktop dialogs use `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`), while mobile bottom action sheets feature `1.5rem` top radii with flush bottom boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#1B4D3E` background, `#FBFBF7` text, subtle upward hover elevation (`translate-y-[-1px]`) and ambient glow.
- **Secondary**: Translucent surface (`rgba(230, 237, 232, 0.8)`), 1px `#D1DED5` border, `#1B4D3E` text.
- **Celebratory (Draw Action / Winner Redemption)**: `#C99A45` background with `#0D2E24` text. Strictly reserved for ticket verification or jackpot claims.
- **Ghost**: Pure transparent body, `#19211D` label with `#1B4D3E` color transition on hover.

### Interactive Number & Ticket Chips
- Circular or pill-shaped tokens housing member entry allocations.
- Inactive state: Soft Sage (`#E6EDE8`) outline and subtle fill with `#19211D` typography.
- Active/Selected state: `#1B4D3E` fill with `#FBFBF7` crisp numerals.
- Drawn/Winner state: `#C99A45` background, high-contrast dark numerals, subtle shimmering perimeter outline.

### Status Badges & Impact Pills
- Minimalist rounded-full tags (`padding: 4px 12px`).
- **Impact Badge**: Soft Sage background (`#E6EDE8`) with `#1B4D3E` dot icon and text.
- **Draw Live Badge**: Pulsing `#C99A45` indicator with light tinted champagne backdrop.

### Cards & Content Modules
- Backdrop-filtered surface with `1rem` internal padding, `1.5rem` border-radius, and crisp 1px `#D1DED5` outline.
- Structural layout divides charity cause data, player metrics, and monthly ticket pools with quiet divider strokes.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- Pill or rounded-lg containers with `#FBFBF7` interior, enclosed by a 1px border of `#D1DED5`.
- Focus state activates a precise `#1B4D3E` border ring with `0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 77, 62, 0.12)`. No harsh browser outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded squircle for checkboxes (`6px` radius); circular for radio buttons.
- Checked state fills `#1B4D3E` with an ivory check/dot indicator.

### Global Chrome (Header, Footer, Sidebar, Bottom Nav)
- **Header**: Sticky glassmorphic bar (`rgba(251, 251, 247, 0.85)` blur), containing logo mark, impact ticker, and subscription profile pill.
- **Sidebar (Desktop Dashboard)**: `#0D2E24` or `#FBFBF7` surface with generous link padding, icon alignment, and persistent entry counter.
- **Mobile Bottom Nav**: Floating glass pill docked with `1rem` margin from bottom screen bounds, housing icon-led navigation points with high hit-target fidelity (`44px` minimum).
- **Modals & Toasts**: Toasts dock at top-center on mobile and bottom-right on desktop; styled as compact glass pills with high-contrast text and explicit action buttons.
