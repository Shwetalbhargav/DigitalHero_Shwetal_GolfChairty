## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the core philosophy: *"Feel, not fairway."* It rejects the clinical, hyper-technical, and exclusionary aesthetics of traditional golf culture in favor of an organic, sensory, and purpose-driven experience. The platform bridges athletic pursuit, communal giving, and tasteful celebration.

### Emotional Landscape
- **Grounded Serenity:** The quiet focus of early morning mist on the green; natural, warm, and inviting rather than sterile or artificial.
- **Dignified Altruism:** Giving is embedded as a core rhythm, presented with reverence and transparency rather than transactional gamification.
- **Restrained Exhilaration:** Celebrations and prize reveals speak in whispered luxury—warm champagne reflections and bespoke editorial typography—deliberately eschewing gaudy casino neon and aggressive dopamine loops.

### Style Archetype: Warm Modern Minimalist with Organic Depth
The visual style pairs soft organic geometry with high-grade editorial typography. Tactile micro-surfaces utilize warm cream foundations, deep chlorophyll shadows, and feathered glass strokes. The interface balances ample breathing space with structured, tabular precision for scorecards and impact metrics.

## Colors

The palette draws straight from untamed linksland, filtered through modern high-end editorial standards. It deliberately bans sterile cold whites (#FFFFFF canvases) and raw neutral blacks (#000000).

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Brand (Rich Grass Green - `#18634B`):** The beating heart of the platform. Used for primary interactions, key metrics, and brand anchor points. Supported by a deep-pressed variant (`#155E46`) for active and hover states.
- **Deep Forest (`#0B2920`, `#081F18`):** The structural authority. Deployed across immersive header cards, premium membership shields, and high-contrast nightfall containers.
- **Accent / Celebration (Warm Champagne Gold - `#CBA358`):** Strictly restrained. Used only for winning indicators, monthly jackpot tags, matching grants, and premium milestones. Never used indiscriminately as decorative tint. Supported by soft highlight (`#E2BF7D`) and deep bronze border tints (`#9A7730`).
- **Canvas / Background (Warm Ivory & Cream - `#FAF9F5`, `#F5F3EB`):** A tactile parchment-like substrate that shields users from screen glare and conveys warmth and heritage.
- **Surfaces & Layers:** Pure White with organic undertones (`#FFFFFF`) layered atop Warm Ivory, contrasted against Soft Sage Green (`#EAF0EC`, `#DFE8E2`) for secondary groupings and utility containers.
- **Typography & Structural Lines:** Primary text rests in Deep Forest Charcoal (`#19211D`), transitioning to Muted Sage Charcoal (`#53655D`) for meta copy and field notes. Structural outlines rely on soft sage tinting (`#E0E6E2` and `rgba(11, 41, 32, 0.08)`).

## Typography

Typography embodies a confident, athletic elegance. **Outfit** provides clean, contemporary geometric displays with a light, warm spirit, while **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers humanist readability for micro-metrics, subscription details, and editorial essays.

### Application Directives
- **Kerning & Tracking:** Display styles require intentional negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to preserve visual density and avoid flimsy, loose headers. Tiny pill labels use expanded uppercase tracking (`+0.04em`) to ensure instant legibility on outdoor mobile screens.
- **Numbers, Scores, and Handicaps:** Numerical data (yardage, strokes, charitable dollars, ticket quantities) must always activate OpenType tabular figures (`tnum`) and diagonal fractions (`frac`) to prevent jitter across dynamic updates.
- **Weight Pairing:** Pair medium/semibold display headlines (`500`/`600`) with regular (`400`) body copy to maintain an editorial balance rather than an overbearing SaaS tech posture.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column grid system (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop) centered within a maximum container width of `1240px` to maintain focused visual cohesion.

### Responsive Rules & Rhythms
- **Mobile Handheld (320px - 767px):** Single-column stack prioritized around one-hand ergonomics. Margins are fixed at `1rem (16px)`, with a minimum target zone of 48px for all interactive points. Navigation and primary CTAs dock into a frosted bottom rail.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 24px margins. Content divides into functional pairings (e.g., Draw Progress Card alongside Charity Impact Feed).
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column asymmetric layout where editorial content and metrics breathe inside wide generous gutters (`2rem`).
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence:** Grouped items within cards utilize `space-sm (8px)` to `space-md (16px)`. Sectional rhythm across cards and draw pools expands up to `space-xl (40px)`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh synthetic dropshadows. Instead, elevation uses light filtered through organic depth: botanical ambient tints, delicate boundary lines, and frosted crystalline surfaces.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Substrate):** The canvas floor (`#FAF9F5`). Completely non-elevated.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Structural Cells):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Soft Sage (`#EAF0EC`) resting atop Ivory with a faint, botanical contact shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 41, 32, 0.05)`. Outlined with a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(11, 41, 32, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Modules, Prize Tiers & Overlays):** Used for elevated draw tickets, active rounds, and subscription selections. `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -6px rgba(11, 41, 32, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(11, 41, 32, 0.03)`. Border shifts to `1px solid rgba(11, 41, 32, 0.1)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Action Dock & Bottom Sheets):** Sticky footers, score-entry sheets, and dialogs. `backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(250, 249, 245, 0.88); box-shadow: 0 -8px 32px -4px rgba(11, 41, 32, 0.12)`.
- **Tier 4 (Celebration & Jackpot Halo):** Used solely for winning states and verified charitable grants. A delicate champagne luminescent glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px 0 rgba(203, 163, 88, 0.25), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(11, 41, 32, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a continuous, soft-pebble curvature. Sharp ninety-degree corners are eliminated to evoke natural topography—undulating fairways, smoothed riverbed pebbles, and ergonomic handheld gear.

### Corner Radii Hierarchy
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** `rounded-2xl (1.5rem / 24px)` on mobile scaling to `rounded-3xl (2rem / 32px)` on desktop displays, framing content like bespoke art paper.
- **Nested Inner Containers (Metric Blocks, Charity Highlights):** `rounded-xl (1rem / 16px)` to maintain concentric harmony with exterior cards.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Tag Badges, Chips):** `rounded-full (9999px)` for pill buttons, touch chips, and data tokens.
- **Spherical Chips (Hole Scores, Ball Markers, Ticket Counts):** Perfect circles (`aspect-ratio: 1/1; border-radius: 50%`) with centered tabular values.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Affordances
- **Primary Action (Join Draw / Submit Round):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height of 52px. Background in Rich Grass Green (`#18634B`), label in White Outfit Semibold (`15px`), with subtle scale reduction on active tap (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Action (View Giving Breakdown):** Warm Sage fill (`#EAF0EC`) with Deep Charcoal typography (`#19211D`) and a hair-thin border (`#DFE8E2`).
- **Celebration / Gold CTA:** Deep Forest Green canvas with Champagne Gold foil border (`#CBA358`) and warm gold text, strictly for entering monthly jackpot draws or claiming matching rewards.

### Chips & Numerical Spheres
- **Status & Entry Chips:** Pill-shaped capsules padding `6px 14px`. Transparent sage tint with crisp green text (`#18634B`) for verified scores; warm champagne background with deep bronze text (`#9A7730`) for winning draw numbers.
- **Hole & Score Badges:** 36px circular tokens with high-contrast text. Birdies and better inherit Rich Grass Green fills with white numerals; bogeys inherit muted sage tinting.

### Cards & Modular Surfaces
- **Charity Partner Card (Impact First):** Warm Ivory canvas surrounded by a soft botanical border. Features human-centric photography with a 16:9 ratio, rounded inner corners (`rounded-xl`), transparent fund allocation meters (e.g., "$12,450 raised for Youth Caddie Scholarships"), and clear non-casino progress visuals.
- **Monthly Prize Card (Participation Second):** Restrained luxury. High-resolution equipment or bespoke travel images framed within Deep Forest surfaces (`#0B2920`). Prize pool stats are stated clearly alongside the charity partner backing the draw.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Rounded pill fields (height 52px) on light cream backgrounds. Border transitions from `#E0E6E2` to Rich Grass Green (`#18634B`) with a soft 3px halo on focus.
- **Subscription Tier Selectors:** Multi-column selectable cards highlighting monthly entries and charitable contributions in tandem. The selected state displays a Rich Grass Green border with a champagne-gold verified checkmark badge.

### Mobile Bottom Dock & Sticky Bars
- Floats 16px above the device safe area with a blurred Ivory backdrop (`rgba(250, 249, 245, 0.9)`), containing round handicap summaries on the left and a continuous primary draw entry button on the right.
