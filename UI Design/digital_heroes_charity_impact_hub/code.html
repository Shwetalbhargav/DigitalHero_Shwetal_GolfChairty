Theory of Change: The Links as Classroom

Founded in 2018, the Fairway Youth Caddie Trust provides structured 16-week training modules pairing teens from historically under-resourced public housing regions with elite caddie masters across Fife and the Lothians.

Beyond green reading and bag carrying, students learn interpersonal mediation, personal financial literacy, and corporate networking. Every hour looped directly vests into an escrow higher education scholarship matching fund.
